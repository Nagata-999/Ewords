whereupon

副詞

    immediately after which; and then as a result

    The alarm sounded, whereupon everyone left the building.

    警報が鳴り、それを受けて全員が建物を出た。

よく使う組み合わせ

  • whereupon he left
  • whereupon the meeting ended
