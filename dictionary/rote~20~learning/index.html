rote learning

音声は端末の読み上げ機能を使用します。

名詞

  1. 丸暗記学習；意味の理解より反復記憶に頼る学習

    learning information by repeating it many times, often without focusing on understanding

    The course combines rote learning of basic terms with discussion.

    その講座は基本用語の反復暗記と討論を組み合わせている。

よく使う組み合わせ

  • rely on rote learning：丸暗記学習に頼る
  • rote learning of facts：事実の反復暗記

Often contrasted with conceptual understanding; rote learning can still be useful for memorizing foundational facts.
