unmarketable

音声は端末の読み上げ機能を使用します。

形容詞

  1. 販売が難しい；市場性のない

    unlikely to be sold successfully because there is little demand or the product cannot meet market requirements

    The damaged goods were considered unmarketable.

    破損した商品は販売できないと判断された。

よく使う組み合わせ

  • unmarketable goods：販売できない商品
  • an unmarketable product：市場性のない製品

Describes goods or assets that are difficult to sell; it does not always mean that selling them is legally prohibited.
