    an important discovery or development that removes a major obstacle

    Researchers reported a breakthrough in battery technology.

    研究者たちは電池技術における画期的な進展を報告した。

  2. 難航した状況の打開；突破口

    a major advance in negotiations or efforts after a period of difficulty

    The two sides finally achieved a breakthrough in the talks.

    双方はついに交渉で突破口を開いた。
