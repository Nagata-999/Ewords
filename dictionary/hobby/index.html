名詞

  1. 趣味

    an activity that someone enjoys doing regularly in their free time

    Photography began as a hobby but later became her career.

    写真撮影は趣味として始まったが、後に彼女の仕事になった。

    Reading is a relatively inexpensive hobby.

    読書は比較的お金のかからない趣味だ。

よく使う組み合わせ

  • take up a hobby：趣味を始める
  • pursue a hobby：趣味に取り組む
  • hobby and interest：趣味や関心事

My hobby is reading. のほか、I enjoy reading in my free time. も自然。
