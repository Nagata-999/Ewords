attract

音声は端末の読み上げ機能を使用します。

動詞

  1. 引きつける；関心を集める

    to cause someone to become interested or to come to a place

    The exhibition attracted visitors from across the country.

    その展覧会は全国から来場者を集めた。

  2. （物理的に）引き寄せる

    to pull something toward something else by a physical force

    Opposite magnetic poles attract each other.

    磁石の異なる極同士は引き合う。
