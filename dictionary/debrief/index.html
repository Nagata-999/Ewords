debrief

音声は端末の読み上げ機能を使用します。

動詞

  1. （活動・任務の後で）報告を聞く；振り返りを行う

    to question or talk with someone after an activity to review what happened

    The team debriefed the volunteers after the emergency drill.

    チームは防災訓練の後、ボランティアから活動内容を聞き取った。

名詞

  1. 事後報告会；振り返りの会

    a meeting in which participants review an event or task after it has ended

    A short debrief helped us identify what to improve next time.

    短い振り返りの会によって、次回改善すべき点が明確になった。

よく使う組み合わせ

  • debrief the team
  • a post-event debrief

As a verb, debrief is often transitive; as a noun, a debrief is a review or discussion after an activity.
