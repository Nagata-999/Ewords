形容詞

  1. まったく同じ；同一の

    exactly the same in every relevant way

    The two groups received identical instructions before the test.

    二つのグループは試験前にまったく同じ指示を受けた。

    Their results were nearly identical despite the different methods.

    方法は異なったが、結果はほぼ同じだった。

よく使う組み合わせ

  • ：～とまったく同じ
  • ：ほぼ同一の
  • ：同一条件

An adjective: identical to is most common; identical with is also used. Nearly identical is common in research.
