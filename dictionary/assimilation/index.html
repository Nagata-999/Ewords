assimilation

音声は端末の読み上げ機能を使用します。

名詞

  1. 同化；吸収

    the process of becoming similar to or integrated into a wider group; also, the absorption of information or substances

    The policy encouraged linguistic assimilation rather than multilingual education.

    その政策は多言語教育よりも言語的同化を促した。

よく使う組み合わせ

  • cultural assimilation
  • assimilation into society

In social science, often refers to minority groups adopting characteristics of a dominant culture.
