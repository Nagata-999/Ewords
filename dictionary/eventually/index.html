eventually

音声は端末の読み上げ機能を使用します。

副詞

  1. 最終的に；やがて

    in the end, especially after a long time or a series of events

    After several failed attempts, the team eventually solved the problem.

    何度か失敗した後、チームは最終的にその問題を解決した。

    After several revisions, the team eventually reached an agreement.

    何度か修正を重ねた末、チームは最終的に合意に達した。

よく使う組み合わせ

  • eventually lead to：最終的に～につながる
  • eventually become：やがて～になる
  • eventually succeed：ついに成功する

Eventually means in the end, not possibly; compare possibly and finally.
