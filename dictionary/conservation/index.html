conservation

音声は端末の読み上げ機能を使用します。

名詞

  1. 自然環境・野生生物の保全

    the protection of natural environments, wildlife, or resources from loss or damage

    Wetland conservation is essential for protecting migratory birds.

    湿地の保全は渡り鳥を守るうえで不可欠だ。

名詞

  1. 資源の節約；保存

    the careful use of resources so that they are not wasted

    Water conservation becomes especially important during prolonged droughts.

    長引く干ばつの間は節水が特に重要になる。

よく使う組み合わせ

  • ：
  • ：
  • ：生物多様性の保全
  • ：保全活動

Conservation usually emphasizes protecting and carefully managing what exists; preservation may emphasize keeping something unchanged.
