7. i Why early gains cannot continue forever

Choose the correct heading for Paragraph G.

Do the statements agree with the information in the passage?

8. FALSE

Chemically resistant molecules are the only long-lasting form of soil carbon.

Evidence: may persist not because it is chemically resistant

鉱物表面等による保護もあります。

9. TRUE

Evidence: loss at depth that cancels a surface gain

本文と一致します。

10. FALSE

The passage states that reduced tillage increases total-profile carbon everywhere.

Evidence: may ... produce little change

一律増加ではありません。

Reading Passage 3 · Questions 27–40

The Politics of Maintenance

Do the statements agree with the views of the writer?

27. NO

Political systems tend to reward maintenance more visibly than new construction.

Evidence: New infrastructure attracts ... political credit. Maintenance rarely does

新設の方が評価されやすいです。

28. YES

Maintenance can change an object’s function and users.

Evidence: adapting ... changes who can use it

保守は継続的再設計でもあります。

29. NO

Delaying a minor repair always increases cost at a constant rate.

Evidence: nonlinear costs

損害は非線形に拡大します。

30. YES

Formal asset systems can omit observations made by local workers.

Evidence: caretaker may notice ... observations may no longer travel

現場知が伝わらない場合があります。

31. NO

Night-time road maintenance eliminates the inconvenience caused by repair.

Evidence: transferring noise to residents

不便を別の人に移すだけです。

32. NO

The author recommends predictive maintenance as the sole method.

Evidence: Combining ... is safer

複数方式の併用を勧めます。
