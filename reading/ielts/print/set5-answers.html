Reading Passage 3 · Questions 27–40

The Argument for Productive Forgetting

Do the statements agree with the views of the writer?

27. NO

A memory system that retained all details equally would necessarily support better decisions.

Evidence: might be less capable of abstraction, updating and choice

完全保持が意思決定を良くするとは限りません。

28. YES

The reconstructive character of memory has both disadvantages and benefits.

Evidence: vulnerable to error, but it also permits old knowledge to be used

欠点と利点の両方が述べられます。

29. NO

Researchers agree that direct inhibition fully explains intentional forgetting.

Evidence: Researchers disagree

研究者の見解は一致していません。

30. NO

Clinical treatment normally seeks to delete traumatic facts completely.

Evidence: not to erase a factual record

治療目的は事実記憶の消去ではありません。

31. YES

New employees may notice assumptions that long-serving staff overlook.

Evidence: New staff sometimes ask basic questions that insiders stopped noticing

本文の言い換えです。

32. NOT GIVEN

All cloud-storage companies now apply automatic time limits to files.

Evidence: Designers increasingly offer deletion controls and time limits

すべての企業が適用しているとはありません。
