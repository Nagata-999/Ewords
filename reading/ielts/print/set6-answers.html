Reading Passage 1 · Questions 1–13

The Return of the Public Drinking Fountain

Choose the correct heading, i–ix.

1. ii An old service given new tasks

Choose the correct heading for Paragraph A.

Evidence: revived interest ... asked to solve problems more complex than thirst

古い設備が新しい課題に対応します。

2. iii The decisive role of location

Choose the correct heading for Paragraph B.

Evidence: Location therefore matters

配置が環境効果を左右します。

3. iv Technology and public confidence

Choose the correct heading for Paragraph C.

Evidence: Trust ... sensor activation ... displays

技術と信頼の関係です。

4. v Seasonal engineering challenges

Choose the correct heading for Paragraph D.

Evidence: Cold climates ... hot regions

季節・気候ごとの技術課題です。

5. i Designing for different bodies

Choose the correct heading for Paragraph E.

Evidence: wheelchair ... children ... low vision

多様な利用者に合わせた設計です。

6. vi Divided ownership and repair

Choose the correct heading for Paragraph F.

Evidence: Responsibility is frequently fragmented

責任分担と修理が主題です。

7. viii Measuring continued use

Choose the correct heading for Paragraph H.

Evidence: Counting installations ... year after year

設置数でなく継続利用を測ります。

Do the statements agree with the information in the passage?

8. FALSE

Every newly installed fountain produces a large environmental saving.

Evidence: depends on use

利用されなければ効果は小さいです。

9. TRUE

Electronic features can create additional maintenance needs.

Evidence: introduce electronics and filters that require specialist repair

本文と一致します。

10. FALSE

The writer says fountains should replace household water provision.

Evidence: supplement, never substitute

置き換えではなく補完です。

Reading Passage 2 · Questions 14–26

What Archaeological Experiments Can Prove

Choose the correct letter, A, B, C or D.

14. They ignored manufacturing sequences.

Why were early appearance-based classifications considered insufficient?

Evidence: produced by different sequences of blows

外見が同じでも製作手順が違うからです。

15. Their relevant properties depend on the research question.

What does the writer say about replica materials?

Evidence: decide which properties matter to the question

研究目的に重要な性質を合わせます。

16. one method could have worked

A successful feasibility experiment demonstrates that

Evidence: one possible method, not the method historically used

可能性の一つを示すだけです。

17. Organisational support may change over time.

Why are long-term projects especially vulnerable?

Evidence: funding ends, staff move and records become inconsistent

組織・資金・人員の変化が影響します。

Which paragraph contains the following information?

18. Paragraph B

an example of experience changing experimental results

Evidence: Skill changes the amount of waste

熟練度が結果を変えます。

19. Paragraph E

a warning that different activities may leave similar evidence

Evidence: overlapping traces

痕跡が重なる場合があります。

20. Paragraph G

a reason to stop an activity despite public expectations

Evidence: valid experiment should be paused

観客向け実演と研究を区別します。

21. Paragraph C

an explanation of when an approximate component is acceptable

Evidence: approximate blade may be sufficient

問いにより近似品で足ります。

22. Paragraph D

a condition for making failed trials useful

Evidence: alternative explanations are systematically reduced

別要因を減らす必要があります。

Reading Passage 3 · Questions 27–40

Why Convenience Changes What We Choose

Do the statements agree with the views of the writer?

27. NO

Any friction in a decision process is harmful.

Evidence: Friction is not inherently bad

安全に役立つ手間もあります。

28. YES

Defaults can sometimes be interpreted as recommendations.

Evidence: can feel like an implied recommendation

本文と一致します。

29. NO

Low prices always overcome platform switching costs.

Evidence: Competitors may offer lower prices yet struggle

低価格でも移行コストが残ります。

30. NO

Providing information guarantees that hurried customers will compare it.

Evidence: Disclosure alone may not protect users

情報提示だけでは十分でありません。

31. NO

Average completion time is sufficient for evaluating public digital services.

Evidence: measure who completes ... abandons ... never begins

平均だけでなく排除も見るべきです。

32. NO

A completely neutral ordering of options can be achieved.

Evidence: Neutral design is ... an aspiration

何らかの順序や初期状態が必要です。
