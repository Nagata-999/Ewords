Passage 1 · 20 minutes · Questions 1–13

The Hidden Science of Wayfinding

1. FALSE

Adding more arrows always solves navigation problems.

Evidence: navigation depends on the relationship between architecture, memory and decision-making

矢印だけでは解決しないという主旨です。

2. FALSE

Survey knowledge usually develops before route knowledge.

Evidence: generally develops only after repeated experience

survey knowledge は反復経験後に発達します。

3. TRUE

Identical corridors may be difficult to remember.

Evidence: offer few memorable cues

記憶に残る手掛かりが少ないと述べています。

4. FALSE

Colour is a dependable solution for every visitor.

Evidence: colour alone is unreliable

every と unreliable が矛盾します。

5. TRUE

Stress can reduce the attention available for navigation.

Evidence: less attention available

本文と一致します。

6. FALSE

Indoor phone navigation is more accurate than outdoor satellite systems.

Evidence: indoor positioning is less accurate

比較が逆です。

Passage 2 · 20 minutes · Questions 14–26

Farming on Water

14. FALSE

Floating beds remain at one fixed height.

Evidence: rise and fall with the water level

固定ではなく水位に合わせて上下します。

15. TRUE

Water hyacinth can create problems in waterways.

Evidence: an invasive plant that can block waterways

本文と一致します。

16. FALSE

Floating cultivation requires no labour.

Evidence: demands substantial labour

no labour と substantial labour が矛盾します。

17. TRUE

The platforms may provide habitat for aquatic life.

Evidence: shelter fish and other aquatic organisms

本文の言い換えです。

18. FALSE

Every type of floodwater is suitable for growing food.

Evidence: contaminated ... or salt can damage crops

every が不適切です。

19. FALSE

The method should be introduced with water hyacinth in all regions.

Evidence: would be irresponsible where ... not already present

全地域への導入を否定しています。

Passage 3 · 20 minutes · Questions 27–40

How Standards Quietly Shape Technology

27. TRUE

Users are most likely to notice standards when compatibility breaks down.

Evidence: notice standards only when they fail

本文と一致します。

28. FALSE

Every standard is created by a government authority.

Evidence: Others emerge through professional bodies, industry groups or widespread adoption

政府以外の成立経路があります。

29. FALSE

The dominant keyboard arrangement is described as technically perfect.

Evidence: without being technically optimal

perfect と not optimal が矛盾します。

30. TRUE

Network effects can make replacing an established standard difficult.

Evidence: A superior alternative can therefore struggle to replace an established ... system

本文と一致します。

31. FALSE

Small firms always have greater influence in standards meetings.

Evidence: smaller firms ... may be underrepresented

影響力が大きいとは逆です。

32. TRUE

Using one design can spread the effect of a flaw across many systems.

Evidence: many systems may be affected simultaneously

本文の言い換えです。

33. NOT GIVEN

The passage gives the ideal number of years between revisions.

Evidence: Standards evolve through revisions

改訂間隔の具体的年数はありません。
