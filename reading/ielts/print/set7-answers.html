Reading Passage 2 · Questions 14–26

The Problem with Measuring Quiet

Choose the correct letter, A, B, C or D.

14. Sound response depends on more than acoustic energy.

Why can equal average levels produce different experiences?

Evidence: pitch, duration, predictability, meaning and perceived control

反応は平均音圧以外にも左右されます。

15. The selected weighting may reduce their numerical importance.

Why can low-frequency complaints conflict with official readings?

Evidence: metric discounts precisely the component residents notice

補正方式が低周波を小さく扱う場合があります。

16. They should not justify avoidable noise.

What caution is given about adding pleasant sounds?

Evidence: avoid using decoration to excuse an unnecessarily noisy source

好ましい音で騒音源を正当化してはいけません。

17. Appropriate low-level sound may be valuable.

What is the writer’s main point about urban quiet?

Evidence: intelligible, appropriate sounds at low intensity

完全無音ではなく適切な音環境です。

20. Paragraph E

an example of participation changing tolerance

相談により受容が変わります。

21. Paragraph F

an explanation of why silence can reduce information

Reading Passage 3 · Questions 27–40

When Expertise Becomes Collective

Do the statements agree with the views of the writer?

27. NO

Modern complex systems have made individual specialist knowledge unnecessary.

Evidence: Expertise has not disappeared from individuals

個人知は残りつつネットワークに依存します。

28. YES

Standardisation can occasionally suppress reports of unusual situations.

Evidence: excessive standardisation can prevent workers from reporting

本文と一致します。

29. NO

Transactive memory requires every team member to learn identical facts.

Evidence: do not all memorise the same facts

誰が知っているかを共有します。

30. NO

Professional diversity always improves decisions without additional effort.

Evidence: not automatically productive

共通言語等が必要です。

31. YES

Formal indicators can overlook valuable practical knowledge.

Evidence: technician ... may understand ... better

本文の例と一致します。

32. NO

Distributed knowledge removes organisational responsibility after failure.

Evidence: Organisations are responsible

むしろ接続設計に責任があります。
