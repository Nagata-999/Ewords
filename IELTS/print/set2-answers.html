Passage 1 · 20 minutes · Questions 1–13

Bringing Urban Streams Back to the Surface

1. NOT GIVEN

All buried urban streams were originally covered to prevent flooding.

Evidence: Waterways were buried so roads and buildings could be constructed above them.

本文は道路や建物の建設を理由として挙げますが、すべての川の唯一の目的までは示していません。

2. TRUE

Modern paved surfaces can increase the amount of water entering a stream.

Evidence: roofs and paved surfaces prevent rain from soaking into the ground

舗装面によって雨が地中へ浸透しにくくなるため、流入量が増えるという説明と一致します。

3. FALSE

Ecological recovery always begins as soon as sunlight reaches the stream.

Evidence: recovery is rarely immediate

always と rarely immediate が矛盾します。

4. TRUE

The new stream increased business for some local shops in one neighbourhood.

Evidence: longer visits by pedestrians increased trade

歩行者の滞在時間が延び、商売が増えたという記述と一致します。

5. FALSE

Repairing an old pipe is always more expensive than daylighting.

Evidence: daylighting [can be] more expensive than repairing an old pipe

費用関係が設問と逆です。

6. TRUE

Residents can contribute information that is absent from official maps.

Evidence: reveal problems that maps overlook

地図が見落とす問題を住民の知識が示せるとあります。

Passage 2 · 20 minutes · Questions 14–26

The Quiet Growth of Seed Libraries

14. FALSE

Every seed library requires users to return seed.

Evidence: encouraged, though not always required

必須ではないため FALSE です。

15. FALSE

Local seed varieties are said to be superior to commercial varieties in all conditions.

Evidence: This does not mean that every local seed performs better

本文が明確に否定しています。

16. TRUE

Tomato seeds are generally easier to save than squash seeds.

Evidence: tomatoes usually pollinate themselves ... squash ... cross-pollinate

自家受粉するトマトの方が保存しやすいという内容です。

17. FALSE

Small seed libraries routinely test every packet for disease.

Evidence: few small projects can test every packet

routine testing とは逆です。

18. TRUE

Workshops can help inexperienced gardeners discuss unsuccessful crops.

Evidence: Novice gardeners also share failures

失敗を共有すると述べています。

19. NOT GIVEN

Commercial seed companies have tried to close all seed libraries.

Evidence: Legal questions have occasionally arisen

法的問題には触れますが、企業が全て閉鎖しようとしたとはありません。

Passage 3 · 20 minutes · Questions 27–40

Why Night Trains Are Returning

27. FALSE

Night trains disappeared entirely from Europe.

Evidence: appeared to be disappearing

完全に消えたとは書かれていません。

28. FALSE

Rail travel always has lower emissions than flying.

Evidence: the comparison is not automatic

always が本文と矛盾します。

29. TRUE

A slower overnight journey can preserve useful daytime hours.

Evidence: save usable time

睡眠中に移動することで日中時間を使えるという説明です。

30. FALSE

All European rail networks use identical signalling rules.

Evidence: different signalling rules

identical と different が矛盾します。

31. FALSE

International night-train tickets are available from every booking system.

Evidence: not always available through every booking system

本文の否定と反対です。

32. NOT GIVEN

Most passengers prefer shared compartments to private cabins.

Evidence: Shared compartments may feel unfamiliar

好みの多数派については述べていません。

33. FALSE

The passage predicts that night trains will replace long-distance aviation.

Evidence: unlikely to replace aviation across very long distances

本文は置き換えないと述べています。
