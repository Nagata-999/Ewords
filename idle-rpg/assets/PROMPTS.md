# RPG v2 visual assets

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Original generated PNGs remain in the generated_images folder. The game uses WebP assets at the same dimensions (quality 92, no resizing), preserving enemy alpha transparency.

- Background: `idle-rpg/assets/stages-atlas.webp` (1448 × 1086, 3 columns × 4 rows; first 10 cells are stages).
- Enemies: `idle-rpg/assets/enemies-atlas.webp` (1270 × 1239, 4 columns × 4 rows; 6 regular enemies and 10 bosses).

## Background prompt

Create ONE production game texture atlas, a precisely aligned 3-column by 4-row contact sheet with 12 equal edge-to-edge rectangular landscape tiles, no gutters, no borders, no labels, no text. Overall aspect ratio 4:3; each tile is 16:9. High quality hand-painted 2D side-scrolling fantasy RPG backgrounds with atmospheric depth, rich lighting, lovingly detailed foliage and stone, sophisticated restrained color palettes, crisp foreground silhouettes and luminous air. Suitable behind simple small chibi characters. Flat side-view walking ground at exactly 80% of each tile's height, open readable battlefield in front, all characters will be drawn by code later so NO creatures, people, weapons, UI or chests. Backgrounds extend seamlessly to tile left/right. Tile order left-to-right top-to-bottom: 1 golden late-afternoon green meadow beside ancient forest, wildflowers, distant rolling hills; 2 emerald dense forest with dappled sunbeams, mossy ancient trunks; 3 turquoise coral coast, shore cliffs and luminous ocean; 4 amber sandstone desert, weathered monumental dunes, ancient sandstone ruins; 5 ancient stone sanctuary ruins lit by violet stars at blue hour; 6 alpine snow pass with ice crystals and snowy firs; 7 volcanic black rock with glowing lava rivers and embers at dusk; 8 heavenly cloud garden with floating stone arches and flowers; 9 gothic twilight castle courtyards, mist, violet fireflies; 10 majestic golden dawn sanctuary with ruined marble pillars and luminous sacred tree; 11 golden autumn woodland; 12 moonlit turquoise ocean cliffs. Every tile should read as an expensive illustrated fantasy game environment, never basic geometric placeholders. These are game textures, not a mockup. Save final image to a local file and return its file path.

## Enemy prompt

ONE production transparent sprite atlas for a polished illustrated 2D side-scrolling fantasy RPG. Exactly 4 columns by 4 rows of equal-sized cells, 16 distinct enemy sprites, no grids, no labels, no text. Each isolated full-body creature occupies its own cell with generous transparent padding and no pixels touching other cells. Creatures face LEFT toward the player. Beautiful hand-painted stylized chibi monster art: dimensional form, exquisite controlled highlights, convincing materials, readable silhouette, detailed faces, soft ink contours; cohesive with painterly fantasy scenery. Cute but threatening, not emoji, not crude polygons, no photorealism. Genuine alpha transparency everywhere outside each creature. Row-major order: 1 mossy green slime with amber eyes, 2 thorny forest spriggan warrior carrying a club, 3 turquoise shell crab with claws, 4 desert kobold raider with scimitar and hood, 5 violet spectral bat, 6 weathered stone golem with glowing runes, 7 boss moss-and-tusk meadow guardian boar, 8 boss ancient tree giant covered in leaves, 9 boss coral armored crab king, 10 boss bronze sandstone sentinel, 11 boss lunar steel armored knight with broadsword, 12 boss towering frost crystal giant, 13 boss red black-scaled magma dragon, 14 boss winged golden lion, 15 boss twilight sorcerer in violet robes with a glowing staff, 16 boss magnificent gold-and-ivory dawn dragon. Every sprite visible in full, including feet and wings; center each sprite in its tile. No cast shadows outside each sprite silhouette. Output a large game sprite sheet, 4 by 4, intended to be consumed directly as a single texture atlas. Save to a local file and return the path.
